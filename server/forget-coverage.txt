# What "Forget my history" (catalog/src/discover/forget.rs) does to each
# table with a column whose name ends in user_id.
#
# One table per line: its name, then one of
#   forgotten     its rows for the person go, in a step of forget.rs
#   kept          they stay; the reason follows on the line
#   not_personal  no data about the person, such as a change counter
#
# persist_schema.rs fails on a table with such a column that is not named
# here, and on a name the schema lacks. A table that holds something new
# about a person adds its line in the change that creates it.

# Discover and playback: what forget removes.
catalog_cached_pages forgotten
catalog_discover_activity forgotten
catalog_discover_picks forgotten
catalog_known_release_groups forgotten
catalog_taste_artists forgotten
catalog_taste_builds forgotten
catalog_taste_groups forgotten
# media_history_imports is cleared to a forgotten mark, so nothing is
# imported until they add a file.
media_history_imports forgotten
media_imported_listens forgotten
media_playback_events forgotten

# What the person told Discover.
catalog_discover_feedback kept: what the person told Discover, listed and undone by them

# Plays and play counts.
library_play_history kept: plays in the app, which play counts and statistics come from
library_user_album_plays kept: the album play counts worked out from those plays
catalog_user_play_marks kept: songs a Jellyfin app marked played or unplayed, which play counts show
compat_bookmarks kept: positions a music app saved to resume from
compat_play_queues kept: the play queue a music app saved
compat_play_queue_items kept: the songs in that saved play queue

# Their library, playlists and marks.
catalog_user_favorites kept: hearts on artists, albums and songs
catalog_user_favorite_names kept: the names shown with those hearts
catalog_user_ratings kept: ratings on artists, albums and songs
catalog_playlists kept: their playlists
catalog_playlist_members kept: playlists shared with them
library_album_looks_wrong kept: albums they marked as looking wrong
catalog_new_release_seen_marks kept: when they last looked at new releases, for the unseen count
catalog_event_seen_marks kept: when they last looked at concerts, for the unseen count
catalog_event_cities kept: the cities they asked for concerts in

# Requests and downloads.
request_history kept: requests, which the library and the request list need
request_history_requesters kept: who asked for each request, and from which page
request_history_dismissals kept: requests they cleared from their list
acquire_discovery_batches kept: their discovery batches, and the page each came from
acquire_followed_artists kept: artists they follow
acquire_held_entries kept: their held downloads and uploads waiting for a decision
auto_download_approvals kept: artists they asked to auto-download, and an admin's answer
personal_mix_approvals kept: their Weekly Mix auto-request, and an admin's answer
download_tasks kept: their downloads
drop_import_jobs kept: their uploads
search_jobs kept: their manual album searches
wanted_watches kept: albums they are waiting for
user_quotas kept: their request quota

# Account, sign-in, links and settings.
auth_tokens kept: their sign-ins
auth_app_passwords kept: their app passwords
auth_password_recovery_codes kept: their recovery codes
auth_providers kept: their single sign-on links
spotify_auth_requests kept: a Spotify sign-in under way, gone when it expires
user_connections kept: their ListenBrainz, Last.fm and media server links
user_listening_prefs kept: their scrobbling and listening settings
compat_user_settings kept: their music app settings
media_navidrome_folder_preferences kept: the Navidrome folders they chose
app_section_prefs kept: which Discover and Home sections they switched off
app_user_appearances kept: their theme and look
app_custom_css_saves kept: their saved custom CSS

# A change counter.
download_activity_user_revisions not_personal
