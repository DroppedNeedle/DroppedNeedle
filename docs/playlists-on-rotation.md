# Lists on rotation

Lists on rotation are five playlists DroppedNeedle makes for you from your own library and keeps fresh. Two are always there, Added recently and On repeat. The other three take turns: every Monday they are swapped for other ideas, and the shuffled ones get new songs. They sit at the top of Playlists, under "On rotation".

## The ideas

Each list is made from one idea. Only your own rows count: your plays here (in the web player and in your music apps), your ratings and hearts, the songs you marked played, and the artists you follow. Nothing is asked of an outside service.

| List | Songs |
|---|---|
| Added recently | The newest 50 songs in the library. Always on. |
| On repeat | Songs you played twice or more in the last 30 days, most played first, up to 40. Always on. |
| Forgotten favourites | Songs rated 4 stars or more, or hearted (the song or its album), that you have not played in 6 months. |
| Deep cuts | Songs you never played from the 20 albums you play most. |
| Five stars | Songs you rated 5 stars. |
| Never played | Songs you have never played or marked played. |
| Artists you follow | Songs by artists you follow that you have not played in the last 30 days. |
| Lossless | FLAC, WAV and ALAC files. |
| A year ago | What you played in the 30 days up to this day a year ago, most played first. |
| The 1990s (a decade) | Songs from one decade. Each time it comes on, it moves to the next decade with enough songs. |
| Long ones | Songs of 7 minutes or more. |

A list needs 20 songs to go on (the two always-on lists need 10). On the Playlists page, the dashed tile beside the lists names the ideas waiting their turn, and those skipped because they have too few songs.

## When they change

Every Monday at 00:00 UTC the three rotating lists are picked again: ideas that were not on lately come first, and ideas with too few songs are skipped. Lists that draw their songs at random (all but Added recently, On repeat and A year ago) draw a new 50 at the same time. Five stars, Lossless, Long ones and the decade keep those 50 all week; Never played, Deep cuts, Forgotten favourites and Artists you follow also drop a song once you play it. The others follow your library and your plays. Every list is brought up to date once a day.

The lists are made for people who used DroppedNeedle in the last two weeks.

## Keeping one, saving a copy

Open a list to see how it is made.

- **Keep on rotation** keeps a rotating list as one of your five after Monday. Its songs still change.
- **Save a copy** makes an ordinary playlist of yours with the same songs, named with today's date, and opens it. You can change that one as you like.

A list on rotation can't be renamed, shared or edited, since the app rewrites it. You can delete one; it stays away until the next Monday.

## In music apps

The lists show in your Subsonic and Jellyfin apps like your other playlists, marked read-only where the app shows that. When a list rotates out, it leaves the app at the app's next sync.

## Turning them off

Turn off **Lists on rotation** in Profile, under Scrobbling & Discovery. That deletes the lists. Turning it back on makes a fresh set within a minute or so.
