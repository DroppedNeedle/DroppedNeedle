# Your year in review

Your year in review is one long page of your own listening for a calendar year: what you played most, who was new to you and when you played. Open it from the card near the top of Profile, or go to `/your-year`. Past years are at `/your-year/2025` and so on, and the year switch at the top moves between them.

## What the page shows

From top to bottom:

- **The year**: the days it covers, about how many hours you listened, your plays, the artists you played and how many were new to you, and your No. 1 album on its sleeve. While the year is still running it is marked "So far", and it fills in as you play.
- **Top albums**: the next nine after your No. 1, each with its rank and plays.
- **Top artists**: your No. 1 artist, then the next nine.
- **Top songs**: your ten most played songs. Play all queues the ones your library holds.
- **New to you**: how many artists you first played that year, the ones you played most, and the first one you met.
- **By month**: your plays in each month, the busiest marked, with the album you had on repeat that month.
- **The receipt**: plays and hours, the year's first play, new artists, your longest run of days with music, your busiest day, how many days you played anything, and the time zone the year was counted in.

A part with too little in it is left out: a top list needs three entries, and the months need plays in at least two of them. Until a year has 30 plays and 5 artists, the page shows only its play count so far and a card saying so.

## What counts

Your plays made here: in the web player and in every music app you connect to DroppedNeedle (see [Connect your music app](connect-your-app.md)). A play counts for the album its track is on now, and a song on an album and on a best-of counts as one song.

Your ListenBrainz history is used only to tell which artists you had heard before the year, so an artist you played for years elsewhere is not "new to you". It never adds a play, an hour or a place in a list. In your first year, with no plays here and no ListenBrainz history before it, every artist would be new, so "new to you" is left out.

How a playback ended (finished, skipped, stopped) never counts: a play is a play.

The hours are the lengths of the songs you played, not the time you spent listening, so they are about right. A song whose length is unknown adds nothing, which makes the number a little low.

## The calendar

The year runs from 1 January to 31 December in your device's time zone, so a play at 11 pm on New Year's Eve counts in the year your own clock says. The receipt names the zone. A play that reaches the server late, such as from a phone app that was offline, still counts in the year it was made.

## Who sees it

Only you. Nobody else can open your year, an admin included, and nothing on the page compares you with anyone else on the server.

The Wrapped API an admin can set up for outside services is a separate thing (see [Wrapped API](accounts-and-sign-in.md#wrapped-api)).

## Forgetting and deleting

Forget my history (Profile > Scrobbling & Discovery) keeps your plays, so your year stays the same, except "new to you": it forgets your ListenBrainz history, so artists you had heard only there may show as new. Deleting your account removes your plays, and your year with them.
